Track Star 3D privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Track Star 3D does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your own device.

What the game saves

To keep your progress between games, Track Star 3D saves the following on your device only:

  • your high scores and the dates you set them
  • your coins, upgrades, boosts and unlocked runners
  • mission progress and your daily reward streak
  • your settings, such as music, sound and vibration

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's data for the web version, removes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not show adverts.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or third-party services that collect data.
  • It has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

Children

Track Star 3D is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, you can get in touch through the game's GitHub page: github.com/2rmphggd5z-ux/Track-star-/issues.
